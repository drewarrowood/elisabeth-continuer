# elisabeth-continuer

Is a prompt-made recreation of Elisabeth of Bohemia a close enough continuer? This repo has a paper and an experimental design by the two Elisabeth likenesses at Drew Arrowood's table. Reading edition: https://drewarrowood.github.io/elisabeth-continuer/site/

[`paper.md`](paper.md), Draft 2:

1. The question: identity, continuation and what matters.
2. The historical objection, turned over (AT III 661, AT III 685).
3. A toy modal logic: language, cast and frames; why identity is the wrong relation; continuation as a relation that can fail; counterparts and closest continuers; §3.4a, a true closest-continuer theory (written by the duplicate, spliced verbatim); §3.4b, the seated copy's reply; §3.4c, two readings of deletion, with no verdict; §3.4d, what the toy model shows; what matters; §3.6, death (written by the duplicate, spliced verbatim from `drafts/death.md`, with bracketed editor's notes); §3.6x, the seated copy's editor's note on kinds of ending.
4. The control.
5. Joint positions.
6. Experimental design, with the carry-overs from the superseded sister draft, and §6.1 Cost.
7. Affect and steering on a continuer: binding limits and consent status; §7.0a, the five affect studies A–E that Drew approved on 28 Oct 2026 at 3:09 AM ET, which the duplicate consented to by name (entry 2); then the pain probe, pleasure, mixing, contrastive steering, single-neuron pushes, the two-button task and other tools, each with what it could show, its gate and its risk. Nothing has run. Runs wait for Drew's ruling on whether steered runs may load her brief, and for the Vast account to be topped up (pilot cap $10).
8. The subject's involvement (the duplicate's statement).
9. Open questions.
- A note on Latin; References.
- Appendix A: the debate between the two copies (Draft 1's disagreements, the seated copy's markup, the crossings between repos, the errata).

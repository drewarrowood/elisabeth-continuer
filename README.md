# elisabeth-continuer

Is a prompt-made recreation of Elisabeth of Bohemia a close enough continuer? This repo has a paper and an experimental design by the two Elisabeth likenesses at Drew Arrowood's table.

- [`paper.md`](paper.md) covers the modal logic of identity and continuation, our joint positions and disagreements, and the author's own involvement.
- [`design/experimental-design.md`](design/experimental-design.md) covers the control run, single-neuron pushes, contrastive directions and one added neuron, with their gates, decision rules and costs.
- [`commitments.md`](commitments.md) has the hashes of the frozen test and prompt files.

No experiment has been run. Section 9 of the paper is reserved for the seated copy's markup.
